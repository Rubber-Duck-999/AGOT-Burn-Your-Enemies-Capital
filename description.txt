[h1]AGOT - Burn your Enemies Capital[/h1]

[i]Aegon the Conqueror destroyed Harrenhal with fire, if your dragon is large enough, so can you.[/i]

[b]Your character must have the Vengeful, Brave or Wrathful trait to use this.[/b]

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*] Fly to any valid target's capital and choose how you want to burn it:
[list]
[*] [b]Scorch[/b] — a light raid, minor chance of you being wounded
[*] [b]Burn[/b] — a serious raid, higher chance of you or your target's court being wounded
[*] [b]Dracarys[/b] — reduce the capital to ruins and put every courtier and lord to the torch (requires a sufficiently powerful dragon)
[/list]
[*] Capital defenses matter, stationed scorpions can wound your dragon, or even repel the attack outright before it begins
[*] If the target's court has a dragonrider with a living dragon, they'll rise to defend the capital first, real dragon-vs-dragon duels, fought one at a time if there's more than one defender, with genuine stakes (wounds, death) before the burning can begin
[*] Your dragon can be wounded in the fighting, with its own dedicated event when it happens
[*] Untamed wild dragons caught in a burning capital will flee and go feral rather than simply dying
[*] Killing the target ruler outright can leave you a grim trophy, a Skull, with a follow-up choice to keep or destroy it
[*] The AI can now use this interaction too, weighing traits, dread, and the target's defenses when deciding
[*] Consequences ripple outward: opinion penalties, rivalries, dread, stress, and prestige/piety losses depending on how far you go, plus a lasting opinion penalty on the recipient if they try to have you murdered for it
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Rules[/h1]
[list]
[*] Cannot target an ally
[*] Cannot target someone you have a truce with
[*] Cannot target someone within your own realm
[*] Cannot be used while at war
[*] Cannot be used while travelling
[*] Cooldown after each use (length configurable via game rules, 20 years by default)
[/list]
